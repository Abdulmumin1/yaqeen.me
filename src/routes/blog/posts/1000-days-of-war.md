<div class="poem">
  <p>
    1000 days of hopelessness, and I only watched <br />
     1000 days of tears, and I smiled all the way <br />
     1000 days of restlessness, and I complained in comfort <br />
     1000 days of mourning, and I remembered no names <br />
     1000 days of bodies, and I scrolled past <br />
     1000 days of injustice, and I was ashamed to speak up <br />
     1000 days of war, and still I was at peace <br />
     Free Palestine
  </p>
</div>
